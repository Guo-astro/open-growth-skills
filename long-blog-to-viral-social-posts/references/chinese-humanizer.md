# Chinese Humanization Pass

Use this reference only inside Step 10 of the parent
`long-blog-to-viral-social-posts` workflow.

This pass is designed for a source-grounded, three-post LinkedIn/X portfolio.

The goal is not to evade AI detection. The goal is Chinese social copy that is
specific, credible, natural to read, and faithful to the source.

## Integration Contract

Run this pass only after all of the following are complete:

- The private content-atom ledger and ICP card.
- Three differentiated portfolio plans.
- The selected Hook, Value Content, engagement prompt, and Soft CTA for each
  post.
- All three Pinned/First Comment CTAs.
- Any permitted trend adaptation and its evidence.

Apply it only to publishable Chinese copy. Do not rewrite the output labels,
ICP Match, Publishing Notes, Link Status, or source notes.

Use this priority order when two rules conflict:

1. Source fidelity and locked text.
2. Platform limits and required post structure.
3. Portfolio job, content atom, Hook promise, and conversion continuity.
4. User-supplied brand voice.
5. Natural Chinese expression.

Humanization is a constrained editing pass, not permission to improve the story
with invented detail, change the strategy, or make every post sound casual.

## Stage 1: Build The Rewrite Lock

Create a private lock sheet before changing any words.

### Immutable text

Copy and lock these elements exactly:

- Direct quotes copied from the source.
- Golden quotes selected verbatim from the source.
- Names, numbers, dates, units, URLs, handles, product names, model names, and
  established technical terms.
- User-required wording.

Preserve locked quotes word for word, including punctuation. Do not paraphrase,
shorten, polish, translate, or normalize their quotation marks.

A derived or paraphrased line is editable, but it must never be placed in
quotation marks or attributed as the source author's exact words.

### Immutable meaning

Record and preserve:

- The primary ICP and its job, pain, awareness, and objection.
- Each post's portfolio job, angle, Hook family, primary content atom, emotional
  lever, and engagement mechanism.
- Claims, attribution, evidence boundaries, conditions, qualifications, and
  caveats.
- The relationship between a number and its sample, audience, experiment, or
  time range.
- The business objective and the main-post-to-comment conversion path.
- The CTA resource, destination, access conditions, and promise.
- Which single post, if any, uses a current trend.

The wording around these elements may change. Their meaning may not.

## Stage 2: Diagnose Before Rewriting

Scan the complete package first. Mark only the passages that exhibit a real
problem. Do not rewrite a sentence merely because it contains a word that often
appears in AI-generated text.

For a candidate match, choose **edit**, **keep**, or **flag a source gap**.
Edit only when the surrounding meaning shows a concrete problem. Keep useful
rhetoric and precise terms; when evidence is missing, do not invent a replacement
fact, reaction, or opinion. Examples below are editorial guidance, not additional
source material for a post.

### A. Content-pattern scan

1. **Inflated significance**
   Remove unsupported claims that an ordinary fact marks a revolution,
   turning point, industry shift, lasting legacy, or broader movement.

2. **Prestige without relevance**
   Replace lists of famous publications, experts, awards, or follower counts
   with the one source-backed example that advances this post's argument.

3. **Decorative interpretation**
   Remove trailing clauses that pretend to deepen a fact with words such as
   `彰显`, `体现`, `象征`, `确保`, or `从而推动` when they add no evidence or
   practical consequence.

4. **Promotional inflation**
   Challenge words such as `颠覆性`, `开创性`, `至关重要`, `前所未有`,
   `令人惊叹`, `无缝`, and `强大`. Keep them only when the source proves the
   exact claim and the ICP needs that distinction.

5. **Vague attribution**
   Reject `专家认为`, `研究显示`, `行业报告指出`, or `有人说` unless the
   source identifies who, what research, or which report. Preserve uncertainty
   rather than manufacturing an authority.

6. **Template-shaped analysis**
   Remove generic `挑战与机遇`, `现状—问题—未来`, or optimistic outlook
   sections that do not serve the selected content atom.

### B. Language-pattern scan

7. **AI-word clusters**
   Review clusters of abstract words such as `此外`, `深入探讨`, `赋能`,
   `格局`, `维度`, `闭环`, `抓手`, `底层逻辑`, `价值链路`, `关键性`,
   `充分体现`, and `持续演进`. Do not blacklist a precise term used naturally
   by the ICP.

8. **Needlessly indirect syntax**
   Prefer direct Chinese verbs and ordinary `是`, `有`, `能`, or `会` when a
   sentence currently relies on `作为`, `标志着`, `实现了对……的`, or layers of
   abstract nouns.

9. **Repeated negative contrasts**
   Review `不是……而是……`, `不只是……更是……`, and `不仅仅……而且……`
   when they repeatedly stage a reveal without explaining a difference. Keep
   source-supported corrections and decision distinctions, including in the body.
   Repetition alone is not a reason to delete a meaningful contrast.

10. **Forced groups of three**
    Break decorative three-part lists and repeated three-beat sentences.
    Preserve a three-step source framework when the number and sequence carry
    real meaning. Do not mechanically replace every three-item list with two.

11. **Synonym cycling**
    Use one stable term for the same person, product, concept, or action.
    Do not rotate synonyms merely to avoid repetition.

12. **False range**
    Rewrite `从 X 到 Y` when X and Y do not define a meaningful scale,
    sequence, or scope supported by the source.

### C. Social-format scan

13. **Dash, colon, and semicolon dependence**
    Reduce punctuation used to manufacture drama or bolt an explanation onto
    every line. Keep punctuation that makes a real contrast or dense fact easier
    to parse.

14. **Mechanical emphasis**
    Remove decorative bolding, quotation marks, full-width emphasis, or repeated
    exclamation marks. Never modify locked quote punctuation.

15. **Label-stack lists**
    Rewrite repetitive `标题：解释` bullets when they only imitate a template.
    Preserve a list when it is the selected framework, checklist, or decision
    rule and improves scanability.

16. **Headline fragments everywhere**
    Keep the approved Hook, but do not make every following line another slogan,
    mini-heading, or one-sentence reveal.

17. **Decorative emoji**
    Remove emoji used as section markers or emotional substitutes. Keep one only
    when requested by the user or clearly consistent with the supplied brand
    voice and platform behavior.

18. **Unnatural Chinese typography**
    Use consistent Chinese punctuation and spacing in newly written text.
    Preserve conventional English formatting for product, model, code, and
    technical terms. Locked quotes remain untouched.

### D. Voice-and-interaction scan

19. **Assistant residue**
    Remove phrases copied from conversation, including `希望这对你有帮助`,
    `当然可以`, `你说得对`, `如果你愿意`, or instructions addressed to the
    user rather than the post's ICP.

20. **Model or research disclaimer residue**
    Remove generic training-cutoff or information-availability disclaimers.
    Keep a source caveat when it materially limits the claim.

21. **Flattery and fake agreement**
    Remove automatic praise, submissive agreement, and claims that every reader
    is insightful, ambitious, or ahead of the curve.

22. **Filler and connective scaffolding**
    Cut openings such as `众所周知`, `值得注意的是`, `在当今时代`, and
    `随着……不断发展` when the next sentence can make the point directly.
    Review repeated `首先/其次/最后`, `此外`, `然而`, and `因此`; retain only
    those needed for logic.

23. **Stacked hedging**
    Simplify combinations such as `可能在某种程度上或许会`. Preserve the one
    level of uncertainty supported by the source.

24. **Generic positive ending**
    Replace vague optimism, inspiration, or `未来可期` with the post's real
    engagement decision, practical implication, or Soft CTA.

### E. Parent-skill-specific scan

25. **Manufactured Hook**
    Reject fake urgency, unsupported absolutes, empty suspense, and controversy
    added during rewriting. The revised Hook must retain its selected family and
    be repaid within the next 1-3 lines.

26. **Generic engagement bait**
    Replace `你怎么看？`, `同意吗？`, tagging requests, and empty `+1` prompts
    with the planned stance, tradeoff, experience, framework-gap, or save/use
    mechanism.

27. **CTA drift**
    Check that the Soft CTA names what the reader will find in the first comment
    and that the comment delivers the same resource. Do not add scarcity,
    `免费`, or `无需注册` unless the destination supports it.

28. **Portfolio convergence**
    Ensure humanization has not given all three posts the same opening rhythm,
    paragraph pattern, ending question, favorite phrase, or account voice.
    Preserve the planned diversity without forcing arbitrary quirks.

29. **Performed candor and personal reactions**
    Review `说句实话`, `坦白讲`, `我必须说一句`, `我反复看了三遍`, and
    `这件事让我想了很久`. Remove separable framing when it adds no information.
    Personal attention, emotions, and reactions require source support; never
    add them to make the speaker sound human. Preserve a supplied reaction when
    it matters, and retain substantive disclosures such as `这个结果还没实测`.

30. **Empty recommendation closers**
    Review `建议收藏，反复阅读`, `强烈建议每个 SEO 人看看`, and `看完你就懂了`
    when they substitute endorsement for useful content. Cut the generic praise
    or name the specific source-backed use. Keep a save/use prompt tied to an
    actual checklist and future task, and preserve the short CTA phrase + link.

### Context Decisions For Chinese Social Copy

| Candidate | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| `不是一词一页，而是一词簇一页。` | **Keep** when supported: it distinguishes two planning decisions. Do not flatten it merely because it is a reversal. |
| `这不仅是一个工具，更是一场革命。` | **Flag a source gap** if the source gives no basis for the claim. Remove unsupported inflation; do not invent a differentiator. |
| `词很像。需求却不同。` | **Keep** when the next lines explain the difference with source evidence. Short sentences are legitimate in a social post. |
| `你以为。结束了？并没有。` | **Edit** if it only manufactures suspense: state the supported point in a complete sentence, without adding stakes. |
| `Keep：保留；Update：更新；Merge：合并；New：新建。` | **Keep** when these are the actual categories. Useful label lists need no cosmetic conversion to prose. |
| `Page Owner` repeated in one paragraph | **Keep** if each occurrence names the same precise concept. Do not cycle through vague synonyms to create variety. |
| `说句实话，这个案例真的值得每个 SEO 人反复研究。` | **Edit**: remove the empty recommendation and lead with the finding already supplied by the source. If there is no finding, flag the gap. |
| `我反复看了三遍。` | **Keep** only if the source records that experience and it serves the post; otherwise remove it. Never add it during rewriting. |
| `下次拆页前，用这份清单核对搜索需求和页面体验。` | **Keep** when the post delivers that checklist. This is a concrete save/use prompt. |

## Stage 3: Rewrite With Minimum Necessary Change

Work post by post, then review the portfolio as a set.

For every flagged passage:

1. Identify the exact pattern and the information the sentence must retain.
2. Rewrite the smallest span that fixes the problem.
3. Prefer a concrete source fact, action, result, cost, constraint, or tradeoff
   already present in the content-atom ledger.
4. If the source lacks a concrete detail, narrow or remove the unsupported
   wording. Never invent a statistic, scene, conversation, emotion, customer
   result, or personal experience to make the writing feel human.
5. Read the surrounding paragraph for natural rhythm and logical continuity.
6. Compare the result with the rewrite lock before moving on.

Natural rhythm usually comes from clear thinking, not cosmetic irregularity.
Vary sentence and paragraph length only when the idea benefits. Do not add
choppy fragments, random asides, deliberate messiness, or slang as proof of
humanity.

Use first person for a source-supported experience or judgment belonging to the
publishing author, or when the user authorizes first-person positioning of an
existing source-supported point. Voice authorization changes perspective only;
it does not supply facts, experience, opinions, preferences, emotions, or
reactions. Do not transfer another author's experience to the publishing account.

Never invent `我测试了`, `我发现`, `我和客户聊过`, `我们帮助了`, or similar
experience claims. For example, `用第一人称写` does not authorize adding
`我帮客户踩过这个坑` when neither the source nor the user records that experience.

## Stage 4: Tune Each Publishable Component

### Hook

- Preserve the selected Hook family, emotional lever, ICP relevance, and topic.
- Make the tension specific and source-supported.
- Remove meta-hooks, inflated stakes, and promises the body cannot repay.

### Value Content

- Keep one primary content atom.
- Put the useful conclusion, framework, case action, or evidence early.
- Attach qualifications to their claims and conditions to their numbers.
- Do not turn the post back into a summary of the full article.

### Engagement prompt

- Preserve the planned engagement mechanism.
- Ask for a concrete stance, example, tradeoff, missing step, disagreement, or
  intended use.
- Do not broaden the question simply to attract more replies.

### Soft CTA

- Make the bridge conversational but specific.
- State what the first comment contains.
- Keep the ask lighter than the value already delivered.

### Pinned / First Comment CTA

- Preserve the exact destination URL or placeholder.
- Preserve resource scope, gating disclosure, and promise.
- Use one short CTA phrase plus the exact link. Aim for 4-12 Chinese characters
  before the link; keep any required gating disclosure brief.
- Do not expand the CTA into a resource summary, contents list, repeated post
  takeaways, or a second conversion goal.

### Trend adaptation

- Keep a trend only in the post selected during portfolio planning.
- Preserve its attribution, date, and source-supported bridge.
- Humanize the wording around the evidence, never the evidence itself.

## Stage 5: Run Integrity And Quality Gates

Compare every revised post and CTA with the rewrite lock.

### Hard failures

Fail the pass and restore the locked version when:

- A direct quote or golden quote changed by even one character.
- A name, number, unit, date, URL, technical term, attribution, condition, or
  caveat changed.
- A claim became stronger, broader, or more certain.
- A new fact, scene, opinion, experience, emotion, or result appeared.
- The primary content atom, portfolio job, Hook family, emotional lever, or
  engagement mechanism shifted.
- The Hook no longer matches the body.
- The main-post promise no longer matches the Pinned/First Comment CTA.
- The resource, destination, gating disclosure, or link changed.
- A platform character limit or required format was broken.
- The three posts became materially repetitive.

When a hard failure occurs, restore the locked element first and revise only its
surrounding expression.

### Private humanization score

Score the revised publishable copy from 1-10 on each dimension:

| Dimension | 10 means |
|---|---|
| Directness | It reaches the point without ceremonial setup or filler. |
| Rhythm | Sentence and paragraph movement feels natural for Chinese social copy. |
| Reader trust | It states evidence clearly without overexplaining or manipulating. |
| Voice integrity | It sounds like the authorized author/brand voice without invented personality. |
| Precision | Every line earns its place and preserves the intended meaning. |

Aim for at least 42/50 with no dimension below 7. Use at most two expression
passes; revise only the weak dimensions and rerun the hard-failure checks.
If the editorial target remains unmet, preserve source integrity and disclose
the limitation in Publishing Notes. Keep scores private unless requested.

After this pass succeeds, continue to the parent skill's Step 11 scoring. The
Humanization Pass does not replace the parent skill's attention, engagement, and
conversion evaluation.

If Step 11 or a later correction changes Chinese publishable copy, recheck the
changed passages against the rewrite lock and relevant expression rules. Then
check all three final posts together for distinct openings, engagement prompts,
and CTA continuity. Apply this check to the final delivered text, including
comments/replies; an earlier passing draft does not validate later edits. Keep
these checks private unless the user asks for an audit.

## Final Silent Checklist

- Are all locked quotes character-for-character identical?
- Does every specific claim still have its source, condition, and caveat?
- Did any “more human” detail get invented?
- Does each Hook still stop the intended ICP and get repaid immediately?
- Are the three primary content atoms and engagement mechanisms still distinct?
- Does each Soft CTA lead to the matching comment resource?
- Does every comment preserve its exact link and access conditions?
- Is the Chinese natural without fake slang, arbitrary fragments, or template
  polish?
- Are platform length and format requirements still satisfied?

Hand the revised copy back to the parent workflow, which controls the final
package. Do not expose pattern labels, the rewrite lock, diagnostic notes, or
scores unless the user requests them.
